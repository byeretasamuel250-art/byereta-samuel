By day, I keep personnel records straight for the Uganda Police — files that have to be findable, accurate, and kept for good.

That work is exacting in a way I've come to appreciate: nothing about a 50,000-file registry forgives sloppiness. It's also what pointed me toward building software — the same instinct for organising information, applied to problems outside the registry.

Outside office hours, I design and run two products of my own, both built for Uganda first.
